# Story rules

Use these rules for the default `vertical-short` profile.

- Write exactly six story lines: each line becomes one five-second scene.
- Keep one complete spoken idea per line. Aim for 4–10 English words; use simple high-frequency vocabulary and concrete nouns and verbs.
- Write original stories with a visible emotional progression: discovery, interaction, shared action, small obstacle or journey, resolution, gentle ending.
- A line must make sense without the image, and the image must show a clear action without relying on on-screen text.
- The Chinese translation is a meaning-preserving line translation, not a word-for-word grammar exercise.
- Each scene must provide `color`, `size`, `taste`, `verb`, and `place`. `taste` may describe an imagined food quality only when it is appropriate to the story; do not make unsafe food claims.

Avoid moral lectures, complex clauses, brand references, frightening conflict, direct calls to buy, and copied story passages.
